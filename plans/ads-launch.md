# Ads launch — decision, campaign spec, copy

**Rule that overrides everything here: Kerb2Kerb moves luggage and goods, never
passengers.** The Growth Toolkit artifact's ad copy predates that rule (it says
"picking you up", "a direct ride", "Airport Transfers") — **do not run it as
written.** The copy below replaces it.

## 1. Which platform first — and why

| | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Meta (Facebook + Instagram)** | **Start here, this week** | The student move-in window (10–13 Sept) is *now*, and Meta is the only platform where you can put an ad in front of "parents, 40–60, Southampton + 20 miles, interested in University of Southampton" for £10/day. It's also where the Reel creative runs natively. UK small businesses sensibly start at £10–20/day ([Scalepoint](https://www.scalepointdigital.co.uk/insights/meta-ads-cost-uk-small-business), [DCB](https://www.dcbresults.co.uk/post/meta-ads-for-small-businesses-uk-guide)). |
| **Google Search** | **Second, once Meta is live** | Cruise customers *search* ("cruise luggage transfer Southampton") — that's purchase intent Meta can't buy, and a national competitor ([Baggex](https://baggex.co.uk/terminals/southampton-luggage-delivery-service/)) proves the search market exists. Google's Local Services Ads need licence/insurance verification first (real admin), so plain Search ads are the quick route. Local-service leads cost roughly the same on both (~£35–45), but Google's convert better per click ([Enrich Labs](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/google-local-service-ads-complete-guide-2026)). |
| **TikTok** | Organic first, boost later | Post the Reel on @jameshickmannn; if one clip takes off, Spark-boost *that* rather than build a from-scratch ad. Cruise audience is small there. |

So: **Meta this week (student + cruise ad sets), Google Search next week (cruise
intent), TikTok organic throughout.**

## 3. Meta campaign spec (enter exactly this)

- **Campaign objective**: Leads → *Messaging* (WhatsApp). Meta will send tapped ads straight into a WhatsApp chat with +44 7926 438553. That's the whole funnel — no landing page, no form.
- **Budget**: £10/day per ad set (£20/day total) for 7 days, then judge. Total risk: £140.
- **Placements**: Facebook + Instagram feed, Stories, Reels. Automatic is fine to start.

### Ad set A — Student move-in (run 4–13 Sept, then pause)

- **Location**: Southampton + 20 miles (catches Winchester, Eastleigh, Fareham, M27 corridor).
- **Age**: 40–60 (parents — they hold the car keys and the wallet). Separate ad set for 18–24 only if A works.
- **Interests**: University of Southampton, Solent University, university life, student life, moving house.
- **Creative**: the "Student move-in special" Reel (film by Friday). Until it exists, a single photo of the van with the headline text works — a real van beats a stock image.

**Primary text** (pick one to start, A/B the other):

> Moving into Southampton halls this week and it won't all fit in the car? I'll take it. One van, door to their halls, £35 flat anywhere in Southampton — you drive down light and it's there when you arrive. WhatsApp me the day.

> Halls move-in is 10–13 September and van demand spikes that week. Kerb2Kerb collects the boxes and cases from your door and delivers them to their new room in Southampton. £35 flat, one driver, one trip. Message me before the slots go.

**Headline**: `Halls move-in: £35, door to door` · **CTA button**: *Send WhatsApp message*

### Ad set B — Cruise luggage (run continuously; ~14–15 sailings a week)

- **Location**: Southampton + 25 miles.
- **Age**: 45–70+.
- **Interests**: cruises, cruise holidays, P&O Cruises, Cunard, Royal Caribbean, MSC Cruises, cruise travel.
- **Creative**: a short clip of cases going into the van and being wheeled onto a terminal forecourt — no people in the van, ever, on screen or in words.

**Primary text**:

> Sailing from Southampton? Your luggage doesn't need to travel with you. I collect your cases from your door and they're at the terminal before you are — you arrive light, no car park, no dragging bags. £12 a case, £30 minimum, agreed up front. WhatsApp me your sailing date.

> Southampton's terminals turn around 14–15 ships a week — I do this run constantly. Kerb2Kerb moves your cruise luggage door to terminal, driven by me personally, insured for goods in transit. Cases collected, delivered, done. Message me for a fixed price.

**Headline**: `Cruise luggage, door to terminal — £12 a case` · **CTA**: *Send WhatsApp message*

### Ad set C — General / Marketplace (only after A and B are running)

The toolkit's Marketplace copy ("bought a sofa and no way to get it home") is fine as-is — it was always goods-only. Price line to add: *"£35 minimum, then by the mile."*

## 4. Google Search (next week)

- **Campaign**: Search, location Southampton + surrounding postcodes, radius bias to the terminal postcodes (SO14, SO15).
- **Keywords** (phrase match): `cruise luggage transfer southampton`, `luggage delivery southampton cruise terminal`, `cruise luggage collection southampton`, `student move van southampton`, `man and van southampton`, `same day courier southampton`.
- **Negative keywords**: `taxi`, `passenger`, `minibus`, `chauffeur` — stops paying for people who want a ride.
- **Headlines** (≤30 chars): `Cruise Luggage, Door to Terminal` · `Southampton Luggage Runs` · `£12 a Case, £30 Minimum` · `Student Move-In Van £35` · `Same-Day Courier Southampton` · `One Driver, Your Bags Only`
- **Descriptions** (≤90): `Cases collected from your door, delivered to the Southampton terminal before you arrive. WhatsApp us.` · `Luggage & goods only, driven personally, goods-in-transit insured. Fixed price agreed up front.`
- Landing page: kerb2kerb.co.uk (the FAQ now answers "can I travel in the van?" — that's deliberate).

## 6. Measure or don't bother

- Every enquiry goes into the Growth Toolkit's **Lead Tracker** with its channel
  (Meta / Google / FB group / referral). After 7 days: which ad set produced
  *bookings*, not clicks. Keep that one, kill the other, move its £10.
- A booking at £35 needs the ad to cost less than ~£12 per lead to be worth it
  at a 1-in-3 close rate. Cruise at £48+ has more room.
