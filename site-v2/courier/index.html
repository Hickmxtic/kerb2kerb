Courier questions

The usual questions.

Is it really direct?

Yes. Your consignment is the only thing in the van from collection to delivery. No depot, no sorting, no other drops on the way.

What can you carry?

Anything that fits in a 2019 Citroën Dispatch and one person can carry — parts, boxes of stock, documents, samples, small machinery on a trolley. No hazardous goods, nothing that needs a chilled van.

Is it insured?

Yes — goods in transit cover up to £25,000 and £5 million public liability, with Somerset Bridge Insurance and Novus Insurance. Everything is photographed at collection and on handover.

Can you invoice my company?

Yes — an invoice by WhatsApp or email on completion, bank payment to settle. No VAT is added.

Do you do regular runs?

Ask. A standing weekly or daily run gets a fixed price and a slot in the diary ahead of one-off requests.

Is same-day always possible?

No — I never promise a slot I haven't checked. Message me, I look at the diary, and you get a yes or a straight no by WhatsApp within the hour during the day.
