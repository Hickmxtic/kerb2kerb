Removals questions

The usual questions.

Do you help carry?

Yes — loading, unloading and carrying to the room are all in the hourly rate. For a sofa, a wardrobe or a fridge I need a second pair of hands at each end: yours, or a friend's.

Can I come in the van?

No — I carry goods only, never people. You make your own way and I meet you at the other end.

Is everything insured?

Yes — goods in transit cover up to £25,000 and £5 million public liability, with Somerset Bridge Insurance and Novus Insurance. I photograph the load at both ends.

How far will you go?

Southampton and around 20 miles — Winchester, Eastleigh, Fareham, Portsmouth, the New Forest, Bournemouth. Beyond that, ask; some longer runs work as a fixed price.

Can you collect from a seller who isn't me?

Yes. Give me the seller's name, postcode and a time they'll be in. I photograph the item before it goes in the van and message you straight away if it isn't as listed.

What if plans change?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours it's a £15 callout.
