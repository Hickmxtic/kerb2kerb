Straight answers

Before you ask.

Can I come in the van with my bags?

No — Kerb2Kerb carries luggage and goods only, never people. You travel however suits you (train, car, taxi) and your bags are waiting for you. That's what keeps it cheap and properly insured.

Is my time confirmed when I send a request?

Not until you get my WhatsApp saying so. It's one van and one driver, so I check the diary first. Book ahead where you can — cruise mornings and move-in weekend fill in the order requests come in.

Is my luggage insured while it's with you?

Yes — goods in transit cover up to £25,000 and £5 million public liability, with Somerset Bridge Insurance and Novus Insurance. I photograph everything at collection and delivery so there's never an argument about condition.

Is VAT added on top?

No. I'm a sole trader under the VAT threshold, so the price you see is the price you pay. Bank payment or cash on completion — no deposit.
