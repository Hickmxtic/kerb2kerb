Parents ask

The usual questions.

Can my son or daughter come in the van?

No — I carry luggage and goods only, never people. They go in your car, or by train, and their things are at halls when they get there. It's what keeps the price at £35 and the insurance straightforward.

What if the time I want is already taken?

I'll offer the nearest one I can do, by WhatsApp, before anything is booked. Requests are taken in the order they arrive, so the earlier you ask the more choice you have.

Do you know where to go at halls?

Yes — I'm in and around the halls sites all week. Forward me the arrival instructions from their accommodation email and I'll work to whatever unloading window they've been given.

Is everything insured?

Yes — goods in transit cover up to £25,000 and £5 million public liability, with Somerset Bridge Insurance and Novus Insurance. I photograph the load at your door and in the room.

What if plans change?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours it's a £15 callout. Move-out in June works exactly the same way — just ask.
